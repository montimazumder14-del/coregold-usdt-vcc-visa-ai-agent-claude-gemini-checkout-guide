## Practical Knowledge: Why Some VCCs Clear AI Billing While Others Fail

### 1. US-Issued BINs & Scenario Matching
Acquiring gateways like Stripe evaluate the issuing BIN profile on every card bind. Low-trust consumer prepaid BINs frequently trigger declines when paired with developer proxies or recurring SaaS subscriptions. **CoreGold provides US-issued card BINs** categorized by actual usage—covering AI tools (ChatGPT, Claude, Grok), Apple Pay / Google Pay, Google Play / Apple Store, and overseas e-commerce, ads, and cloud services.

### 2. Strict AVS (Billing Address) Alignment
North American merchants verify whether your checkout billing address matches the card's registered record. On CoreGold, you can keep **Auto-Generate Billing Info** checked or manually enter Name, State, City, Street Address, and ZIP Code. Always copy that exact billing address verbatim when checking out at merchants.

### 3. Hardware Tokenization via Apple Pay & Google Pay
All CoreGold card BINs can be bound to **Apple Pay** and **Google Pay**. Paying through device wallets replaces raw card numbers with hardware-backed tokens (DPAN) and biometric confirmation, reducing web-form friction on supported checkouts.

### 4. Card-Level Balance & Freeze Controls
Using **Card Management**, you can check card numbers, expiration dates, CVVs, balances, and status in one place, and top up, withdraw, or freeze cards anytime to prevent unwanted recurring charges.

## Simple Step-by-Step Setup Tutorial

### Step 1: Register on CoreGold
Visit [https://app.coregold.io/signup?invite_code=ADvlj9b](https://app.coregold.io/signup?invite_code=ADvlj9b), register using your regular email address, and log in to the user dashboard.

### Step 2: Deposit USDT via TRON (TRC20)
1. In the left menu, click **My Wallet (我的钱包)** -> **Deposit (充值)**.
2. Enter your deposit amount to display the USDT deposit address and QR code.
3. Open your crypto wallet or exchange (such as OKX or Binance; if you don't hold USDT yet, you can purchase it there first), select USDT withdrawal/transfer, and choose the **TRON (TRC20)** network.
4. Copy the deposit address or scan the QR code, verify the address, network, and amount, and confirm the transfer. Funds credit automatically after transfer (contact customer support if not credited within 10 minutes).

### Step 3: Select Plan, Card Category & Billing Details
1. Click **Apply for Card (申请开卡)** in the left menu and choose an **Individual Plan** (such as Diamond Plan or AI Subscription) or **Team Plan** (check live page details for current pricing, supported scenarios, and fees).
2. Select the matching US-issued card BIN category and enter the initial deposit amount.
3. Keep **Auto-Generate Billing Info** checked, or uncheck it to enter Name, State, City, Street Address, and ZIP Code manually, keeping billing addresses consistent during merchant checkout.

### Step 4: View Card & Bind Apple Pay / Google Pay
1. Open **Card Management (卡片管理)** and select your card to view card number, expiration date, CVV, available balance, billing address, and status, or use top-up, withdrawal, and freeze controls.
2. To link **Apple Pay** or **Google Pay**:
   - Open **Apple Wallet** on iPhone or **Google Wallet** on Android, select **Add Payment Card**, and enter the card number, expiration date, and CVV manually.
   - Select **email verification** when prompted.
   - Return to CoreGold **Card Management**, open the card details page, and click **Add to Digital Wallet (添加到数字钱包)** at the bottom to view the verification code.
   - Enter the verification code back into your mobile wallet to complete binding.
